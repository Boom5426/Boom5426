## 👋 About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher working at the intersection of **artificial intelligence and biology**. My research focuses on building and evaluating computational systems that can understand cellular responses to perturbations and support better intervention decisions.

- 🧬 **Virtual Cells** — predictive and decision-oriented models of cellular response
- 🧫 **Single-cell Perturbation Modeling** — genetic and drug perturbations across cellular contexts
- 💊 **Inverse Intervention Design** — selecting interventions that drive cells toward desired states
- 🖼️ **Multimodal Biology** — gene expression, Cell Painting, spatial and other cellular measurements
- 📏 **Evaluation & Measurement** — understanding what benchmarks and experiments can actually resolve
- 🤖 **Scientific Agents** — tools and agent workflows for research, analysis, and scientific writing

> I care less about winning a benchmark by a small margin, and more about whether a model changes a scientifically meaningful or experimentally actionable decision.
